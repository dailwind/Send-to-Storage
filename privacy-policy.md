# Privacy Policy for Send to Storage

**Last updated:** September 2026

Send to Storage is built as a utility tool to help users organize and save shared files locally on their devices.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
Send to Storage is a 100% offline application.
* We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, device data, or user files.
* All files processed by the app remain strictly on your local device or connected external storage.

### 2. Storage Permissions (SAF)
The app uses Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF) to write files only to the specific folders explicitly chosen and authorized by you. We do not access, view, or scan any files outside the directories you select.

### 3. Third-Party Services
The app does not contain third-party SDKs, analytics tracking tools, or advertising networks.

### 4. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact the developer via the email provided on the Google Play Store listing.
